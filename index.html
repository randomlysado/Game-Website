Welcome to Space Defenders
A game where you play as a Earths protector defending it from alien invaders.
You pilot your space jet and fire off energy bullets to defeat them.

Enemies become stronger with every level.
They gain new abilities:
Increased damage
Increase Health
More Enemies can attack at once
Greater number of Reinforcements

These abilties change every level and the number of abilities increase endlessly


!!!! BE CAREFUL SOLDIER !!!

You can take 5 hits before you lose the game
Enemies require minimum of 3 shots before they are defeated
(Remember their health can change each level)

Be careful of their charge attack, a direct hit from that does double their regular damage
